# 5. Calm motion, a press-and-hold glass, and letters that gather
Date: 2026-10-16
Follows: 0004-round-3-home-scroll-only-socrates.md.

Context: The owner's notes on version 6:
- "in the reader, Socrates facer goes from higher definition to more intelligible. Fix this"
- "I don't like the zoom, how it automatically appears as you move your mouse around on the library. It's distracting"
- "it'd be a lot cooler if the letters in the library were moving around beforehand, then when you scroll, they gather to create the face."
- "Remember when I say movement it has to be calm"

Decision:
- All movement on the site is calm: slow, eased, and never following the pointer uninvited. This is now a design principle.
- The reader's Socrates emblem is spelled in capitals with no spaces or accents, as Greek was written in Plato's time. Its picture is sharpened, and it no longer blurs once it has settled. While it gathers, its letters change slowly rather than flicker.
- In the library, the reading glass appears only while you press and hold on the face.
- Before you scroll, the Apology's letters wander slowly across the library's stage. Scrolling gathers them into the face. The ring of letters that circled the stage is removed.
- Once the face has gathered, the passing band of light lights only the face, not the empty dark around it.

Consequences:
- Emblems in letters use capitals wherever their text allows it. A small face drawn in accented lower case loses its features.
- Any later effect that reacts to the pointer must ask for a deliberate action (press, hold, or a slow fan) before it appears.
