# 1. Visual direction for the redesign
Date: 2026-10-15

Context: The owner asked for a fresh-start redesign. The site should feel beautiful, mysterious and insightful: warm colours, dark mode, texture and noise, dimly lit rooms. Their model is the Nous Research Hermes Agent website, for its animation and feel. It must serve both newcomers and experts, on phone and PC. The home page has to draw people in, and the reader has to be calm.

Options (round 1 samples, all with real licensed images):
- A, Red-figure: a halftone vase in black and terracotta. Strong, but its painted look was the least mysterious.
- B, Oracle: Socrates drawn in Greek letters, lit by an amber lamp. The most atmospheric, and the calmest reader.
- C, Engraving: an Athenian coin engraved in gold lines on Pompeian red. The most striking way in.

Decision:
- The home page follows C, and the reader follows B. B's lamp-lit Socrates goes in the library, spelled out of the Greek of Plato's Apology.
- Each text gets its own emblem, drawn in Greek letters: the author's face, the thing the text is best known for, or a theme.
- The site is dark only. A light page exists only inside the reader, as a reading aid.
- Every page has interactive art except the reader.
- Each page has its own scroll effect: a black page with windows onto art behind it, moving at different speeds.
- Round 2 offered three ways to scroll: Windows, Shutters and Stage. The owner's pick will be recorded in `DESIGN.md` (Motion).

Consequences:
- Every text needs a licensed picture and a credit before it gets its emblem. Until then it needs a fallback emblem, such as a theme drawn from the text.
- Canvas art has to stay light on phones, and has to hold still when "reduce motion" is on.
- Dropping light mode removes the current site's light theme. Only the reader keeps a light page.
- The display, reading and Greek fonts are all under the Open Font Licence and carry full polytonic Greek. They replace GFS Didot, Alegreya and Alegreya Sans SC.
