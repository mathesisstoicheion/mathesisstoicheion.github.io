# SPEC.md — Mathesis Stoicheion · Μάθησις Στοιχείων

Agreed with the owner on 2026-10-17, updated 2026-10-18. This file decides what the site is. Where `PLAN.md`, `Handoff.md` or the
brief (`greek-reader-website-prompt.md`) disagree with it, this file wins. Terms are defined in `CONTEXT.md`.

## The site in one line

**Read real Ancient Greek from the first week, with every word explained on the line.**

## Who it is for

An adult teaching themselves Ancient Greek, past the alphabet, who wants to read Plato or Homer without a class.

## The moment it must win

The reader clicks a word and sees its meaning, its form and the LSJ entry in place, without leaving the
sentence. Every other feature exists to bring a reader to that moment or to help them understand it.

## What stays and grows

- **The reader** (the Mouseion) with the word look-up. This is the heart of the site.
- **The Academy** (Learn), but only as a path into real texts: every lesson ends with "Read it for real".
- **The Oracle** (search), offline reading, the phone edition.
- **The Treasury** (Mine), as long as it serves reading: saved words, notes, bookmarks.

## Frozen: kept as it is, nothing new added

- **The Town Hall and the Pnyx** (forum and debates): hidden everywhere, data kept, for now.
- **Accounts stay**: the owner signs in to open her private texts. Sign-in keeps working while the forum is hidden.
- **"Talk where you read"** (Phase 11, idea 5): dropped.
- **The Periplus** (map) and the Painted Stoa's everyday-life entries: kept at what exists.

## Not doing

- **The 8,000-text import** from another Greek reader site.
- **Author drafts** whose texts are not in the library. Only drafts for authors the library holds are checked
  and published.
- **Showing the site to scholars** (the comparison with Lady Babylon and Perseus, the message to Dr. Hillman): cut
  until the site is finished and refined.
- **The old website** (Μάθησις, `ladygagastani.github.io/Mathesis`, and the `Documents/Mathesis` and
  `Documents/docs-mathesis` folders): scrapped completely (owner, 2026-10-18).

## A separate project

**The Ancient Greek translation tool** has its own folder, `Documents/greek-translation-tool`. It is planned to join
this site later as a reading aid. Until then nothing of it is built here.

- Texts and translations are shown exactly as the sources give them. Every fact needs a source.
- Uncertain parsings and scansions are marked, never guessed.
